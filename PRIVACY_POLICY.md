# Privacy Policy for SubShelf

**Last Updated:** September 5, 2026

SubShelf ("we", "our", or "the extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user data.

---

### 1. Zero Data Collection
SubShelf operates entirely on your local device. 
* We do **NOT** collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal information.
* We do **NOT** collect your Google account credentials, passwords, email address, or browsing history.
* We do **NOT** operate any external tracking servers, analytics endpoints, or advertising networks.

---

### 2. Local Storage Usage
SubShelf uses `chrome.storage.local` solely to store:
1. Category deck names, icons, and IDs created by you or generated by the local categorizer.
2. Channel UC IDs and handles required to display channel lists in your sidebar folders.
3. User preferences (such as AI model selection and the "Hide Shorts" toggle).

This data never leaves your computer and can be cleared at any time by uninstalling the extension or clearing extension data.

---

### 3. Optional AI Features & API Keys
* **On-Device AI (Gemini Nano):** If enabled, channel categorization is executed completely on your device using Chrome's built-in AI models. No data is sent over the network.
* **Cloud API (Google Gemini):** If you optionally provide your own personal Google Gemini API key, channel titles are sent directly from your browser to Google's official Gemini API endpoint for categorization. We do not intermediate, proxy, or retain these requests.

---

### 4. Third-Party Services
SubShelf interacts only with YouTube™ (`*://*.youtube.com/*`) in your active browser session to read channel names and apply feed filters. It does not communicate with any third-party websites or services.

---

### 5. Changes to this Policy
If we update this Privacy Policy, the revised version will be posted in this repository.
